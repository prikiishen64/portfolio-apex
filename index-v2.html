Frequently Asked Questions

Do we need to buy licenses separately?

No, we act as your direct reseller. We provide the licenses for Microsoft 365 and Sophos, set them up, and manage the renewals so you only have to deal with one trusted partner.

Do you provide hardware like servers or routers?

We focus 100% on Cloud Productivity and Cybersecurity software. By keeping your infrastructure in the cloud (like SharePoint and OneDrive), you eliminate the expensive need for local server hardware.

Why do we need a Cloud Backup if we use M365?

Microsoft guarantees the infrastructure, but they do not guarantee your data against accidental deletion by employees or targeted ransomware. Our third-party cloud backup ensures your data is retrievable no matter what happens.
